HOTSPOT EDITOR ⇄ dock left

Draw: click on the image to drop points (in order, around the edge).
Insert: click on a line segment to add a point between two others.
Move: drag a point.
Delete point: right-click it, or select it & press Del.
Redraw from scratch: Clear points (or press C), then click out the new shape.
Switch object: click it in the list, or click any of its points.
Zoom: the ＋ / － buttons, +/-/0 keys, or ⌘/Ctrl + scroll. Drag-scroll (or trackpad) to pan when zoomed.
Edits auto-save in this browser. E exits the editor.
