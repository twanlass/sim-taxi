Rocket Rides Privacy Policy

Last updated October 6, 2026

Rocket Rides does not collect, store, or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the iOS app makes no network connections at all.

What stays on your device

The game saves a few things locally so it can remember them between sessions: your high scores and the initials you enter with them, your sound and music settings, and which tutorial tips you have already seen. This data never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Children

Because the game collects nothing, it collects nothing from children either.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the app that changes it is released, and the date above will move.
