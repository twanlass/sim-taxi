Rocket Rides Support

Found a bug, stuck on something, or have an idea? Email rocketrides@48made.com. It helps to include your iPhone model and iOS version.

How to play

Tap a waiting rider to send the taxi to them, then get them to their stop before their clock runs out. Hold Loco Mode to boost, tap the brake to slide into corners, and double-tap the brake while boosting to spin a U-turn.

My scores disappeared

Scores are saved on your device only. Deleting and reinstalling the app clears them.
